# 行为准则 / Code of Conduct

## 简体中文
我们希望「我的绿洲」是一个友善、包容的社区。
- 尊重每个人，不论年龄、性别、民族、国籍、信仰、性取向、残障或经验水平；
- 就事论事，友善地给出和接受意见；
- 禁止骚扰、人身攻击、歧视、色情内容、公开他人隐私；
- 维护者可以删除不当内容、拒绝贡献，或暂时/永久禁止违规者参与。

遇到问题请通过 GitHub 私信或私密方式联系维护者 @muoushikoma。

## English
We want My Oasis to be a friendly, inclusive community.
- Respect everyone regardless of age, gender, ethnicity, nationality, religion, sexual orientation, disability or experience;
- Discuss ideas, not people; give and accept feedback kindly;
- No harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, sexual content, or doxxing;
- Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, reject contributions, or ban participants temporarily or permanently.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer @muoushikoma privately.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
